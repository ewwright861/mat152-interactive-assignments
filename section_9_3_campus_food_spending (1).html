The situation

What does a typical student spend?

A campus survey claims that college students spend an average of $60 per week on coffee, snacks, and takeout. You suspect the actual mean is higher. A random sample of 36 students reports the following:

Claimed mean, μ₀$60
Sample size, n36
Sample mean, x̄$67
Sample SD, s$18
Significance, α0.05

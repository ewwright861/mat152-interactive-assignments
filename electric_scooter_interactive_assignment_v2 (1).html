Electric Scooter Charging Times

Interactive Hypothesis-Testing Assignment · Critical Value Method

Investigate the claim

A company claims that its electric scooters have an average charging time of 6 hours. A researcher believes the average charging time is less than 6 hours. A random sample of 64 scooters has a mean charging time of 5.6 hours. The population standard deviation is known to be 1.6 hours. Use a significance level of α = 0.05 to test the researcher's claim using the critical value method.

Sample size64
Sample mean5.6 hr
Population σ1.6 hr
Significance level0.05

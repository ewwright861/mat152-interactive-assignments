Electric Scooter Charging Times

Interactive Hypothesis-Testing Assignment
Critical-Value Method & P-Value Method

Investigate the Claim

A company claims that its electric scooters have an average charging time of 6 hours. Two researchers investigate the company's claim using different random samples.

In Part 1, you will use the critical-value method. In Part 2, you will use the P-value method.

Both investigations use a significance level of 0.05 and a known population standard deviation of 1.6 hours.

Claimed mean 6 hr
Population σ 1.6 hr
Significance level 0.05
